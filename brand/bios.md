## Bio: long (~300 words)

Shobhit Tripathi is an engineering leader who builds rigorous, AI-native platforms end to end. As CTO / IT Director at NS Engineering, he designed and runs the company's entire technology stack as its sole engineer: identity and single sign-on, private networking across environments, CI/CD built from zero, production monitoring, and an ERP that supports ISO/IEC 17025 lab compliance. AI-assisted engineering is the default way he builds, not an experiment. Before that he spent 7+ years at Deloitte, where he led the team behind a firm-wide inter-firm agreement system, owned the Kubernetes layer of a 99.9%-uptime microservices platform, and earned Technology Guild Guru, the firm's highest internal recognition for blockchain expertise. He also builds Quantum Foundry, an open-source multi-physics simulator for post-silicon transistors, built on one rule: never report a number a solver didn't produce.

His work sits where the hard part isn't only the code: identity systems that must hold up under audit, lab software that has to meet an international quality standard, and physics software where a plausible-looking wrong number is worse than no number. He owns systems end to end: he designs them, builds them, deploys them, watches them in production, and fixes them fast when they break. At Deloitte he also ran delivery as Scrum Master and primary client-facing technical lead for US-based stakeholders, mentored 50+ engineers through the firm's technology guild, and founded and hosted an internal engineering podcast that grew to more than 500 listeners.

He holds an MCA from Vellore Institute of Technology and is based in Kathmandu and open to remote work worldwide.
